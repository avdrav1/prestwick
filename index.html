Prestwick

Build a golf course, then play it. Guests play it too, pay a green fee, and tell you what they think.

Playtest build 0.6.0-rc4
Download for Windows Download for Linux Example course

One file, nothing to install. Windows may warn about an unknown app: choose More info, then Run anyway. On Linux, run chmod +x prestwick-0.6.0-rc4-linux.x86_64 first. The example course opens with Import course in the menu (put the file in the folder the Import page's Open folder shows).

How to play

  • Goal: build a nine-hole course that earns 3 stars without running out of money. You start with $5,000.
  • Build: click New hole, place a Tee and a Cup, paint a green round the cup and fairway between. The panel at the top right says what is wrong until the hole is legal; then Open it. Painting and shaping cost money; undo gives it back.
  • Guests: each morning they leave the clubhouse, pay at the first tee and play your open holes. Their comments show on the left and on the map; click one to jump to that hole. At dusk their verdicts set your stars, and more stars bring more guests.
  • Cart paths: guests walk half again as fast on a path and slower through trees, sand, water and up hills, and a long walk from a green to the next tee with no path draws a complaint, so lay paths where the walks are long.
  • Play a hole yourself: Tab switches Build and Play. Aim with the mouse or arrow keys; swing with Space or a click: once to start, again for power, again for accuracy.

Tell us what happened

Play for half an hour or so, then open a playtest report (needs a free GitHub account). Short notes are fine: what confused you, what broke, what you liked. If you made a course you're proud of, export it and attach the file.

All builds and notes: github.com/avdrav1/prestwick/releases. Art and sound are generated by scripts in the repo.
