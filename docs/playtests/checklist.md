# Full playtest checklist

1. Start a new sandbox. Build a par 3 under $1,500. Does the validator tell you what is wrong before it passes?
2. Play it. Did the ball land where the aim line said? What did the swing bar feel like?
3. Open it. Watch three guests. Did their reactions match your own?
4. Build a par 5 with water on the direct line. Does the cost feel fair?
5. Play it twice: once laying up, once going for it. Was the choice real?
6. Run two days at 3x. Could you read the feed?
7. Set the fee at the ceiling. Did arrivals drop in a way you noticed?
8. Save, quit, load. Anything lost?

After each item: what surprised you?
