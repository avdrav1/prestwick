# E6-S8 scripted playtest: a start on land (2026-11-03)

Scripted, not by hand: `scripts/land_playtest.gd` drove the real main scene at a fixed 60 fps on branch `swarm/e6-s8` (base bd54fb0, every E6 story up to E6-S7 landed). It plays the way a player would, through the HUD and the tools. A New course on a seed, the Buy land tool and its confirm for the cheapest two parcels in a line off the starting block, a par 4 built with the palette (tee box, a 5-wide fairway, a green, tee and cup tools, the par button, Open), then Play with the scripted player (`scripts/scripted_round.gd`, a perfect swing chosen through `ShotPrediction`) until the ball holes out. Rerun with `godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 -s res://scripts/land_playtest.gd -- --seed 1`.

The scripted player cannot say how anything felt. The Felt section lists what it measured that the user should judge by hand.

## D29's reading: OB on a roll that would have come back

From each tee, every full club at every scripted power (7 powers), aimed every 3 degrees within 60 degrees of the cup, was predicted on the course and on a fully owned copy of it.

| Seed | Strokes | OB on unowned land | Touched down on owned land, rolled over the line | Of those, would have rolled back to owned land |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3,157 | 832 | 59 | 1 |
| 2 | 3,157 | 910 | 66 | 0 |
| 7 | 3,157 | 769 | 47 | 0 |
| 42 | 3,157 | 1,180 | 109 | 0 |
| All | 12,628 | 3,691 | 281 | 1 |

The one case: seed 1, a 4-iron at 64 ticks aimed 30 degrees right. It lands on (72, 49) in the block, crosses into parcel (4, 2) at (73, 47), and with no line it would have stopped at (73, 48), one tile back inside. On generated land (one step every 2 tiles at most, E6-S2) a ball that rolls over the line almost never comes back. So D29's reading, "OB as an `oob` tile is", gives the same result as "OB where it comes to rest" in all but 1 of these 12,628 strokes. That can change once the player raises steep ground beside the line.

## Felt (for the user's hand playtest)

- Is one parcel (80 yd) wide enough for a par 4 corridor? With the line 40 yd either side of the fairway's middle, 29% of the swept strokes (3,691 of 12,628) end OB. That count includes strokes aimed 60 degrees off line.
- Does the parcel price tell you anything? Hills cost more, but woods and ponds barely show in the price (see Balance below).
- Does an OB at the land line read as fair when the ball rolled over it? The data above says it almost never matters on generated land.

## Balance

- `/balance land` (seeds 1 to 200) hit every target with `data/land.json` unchanged. The budget buys 4 to 5 parcels, there is room for a par 3 and a par 4 on every seed, and room for nine holes on none (`docs/design.md` Land, Measured).
- On three of the four seeds a perfect drive (driver at 64 of 72 bar ticks) reaches the green of the 280 yd par 4, and two of those holed in 2. This is a clubs and validator question, not land: rule 4's shortest par 4 (250 yd) is shorter than a perfect drive's total. The scripted player always swings perfectly, and guests in E3 will not. Open: not filed in `docs/BACKLOG.md` by this story. It is reported to the orchestrator as an E6-S8 followup for a `/balance` story on the clubs table (`golf/clubs.tres`, driver carry and roll) or validator rule 4 (`course/validator_tuning.tres` `par4_min_yd`), to settle before E3 rates par.
