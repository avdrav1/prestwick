## The two open points (for the user, not decided)

Measured on the tuned table (money scale 0.63, `docs/decisions/e4-s8-balance-economy.txt`):

1. **A good course outruns the pacing target.** A 4.5-star fair course (70% praise, 27% neutral, 3% complaints) never stalls for money and opens its nine on day 17, lowest balance $2,027 (day 17 at 0.61 too). For it to open on day 30 the prices would have to be about 1.09 of the T4 table, against 0.62 for a 3-star course (the options pass, income I5, which is the chosen economy). The 3-star course opens on day 30 after 13 days of stall. So with one table the target holds for exactly the 3-star course the target names; a better course builds faster, by up to 13 days.
2. **The 24-guest cap and D46's slow-foursome margin.** `scripts/guest_day.gd -- seeds=1,2,3,4,5 quick arrivals=24` on the nine-hole fixture (nothing in it moved with the tuning, so it is E4-S5's run again; `docs/decisions/e4-s8-guest-day-24.txt`): unchanged, 52 of 53 runs finish. Every group finishes on each of the 5 drawn days (latest 505 s, day 3), and 47 of the 48 runs with a skill-1 foursome put in each arrival slot in turn. The one that does not is day 3 with the foursome as group 1: 8 of 9 groups finish, one ends short at dusk (540 s). The closest that holds is day 1 with the foursome first, 537 s, 3 s inside dusk. On PlaytestNine (`/balance economy`'s capacity rows, 3 days) 24 a day finish 72 of 72, and 32 leave 15 of 96 out at dusk. The tuned table does not change who comes at the cap: 24 a day still needs 4 stars or more at a fee at or under 70% of the ceiling.

## Findings

1. **The stars shown swing within a day, and the fee follows them down for good** (filed: Icebox, top, pillar 3). On the nine, about 160 reactions a day turn the 50-reaction window over about three times, so the stars shown moved 2.5 to 3.5 on days 1 and 2 and 2.0 to 3.5 on day 3. E4-S6's rule lowers the fee whenever the ceiling falls under it and leaves it when the rating recovers: the fee left at the $60 start fee fell to $50 at second 148 of day 3 (a dip to 2 stars) and stayed there with the course back at 3.5 stars; on day 4 a fee at $87 fell to $62 by second 150. A player who never touches the slider loses $10 a round from one bad hour. `/balance economy` works a day at a time and cannot see this; its "fee left at the start fee" run opens the nine on day 30 as if the fee held. As designed (E4-S6), so a feel problem, not a bug: a question for the user, in the hand checklist.
2. **A new course's first plain hole costs it half a star by day 2.** The checklist's basic par 3 (nothing on the line) draws 0 praise ("nothing in the way, nothing to think about") and 13 complaints in 31, so the rating is 2.48 on the morning of day 2 and 13 guests come, not 18. That is the reactions working (pillar 3), and `/balance economy` paces a 3-star course; recorded here, not filed. It bears on open point 1 (the rating a player's course actually has sets the pace).
3. **A load counts one reaction twice in the feed's total** (filed: E4-S12). After the item 8 load the replayed day 4 ends with `ReactionFeed.total` 658, the uninterrupted day 657; balance, rating history, fee and course agree. `ReactionFeed.to_save` takes off the saved day's reactions still in the window (`dropped`), not the ones the day had already pushed out of it, so a save made after the day's own reactions passed the 50-reaction window keeps those in `total` and the replay counts them again. Nothing the player sees reads `total` today, so it shows nowhere yet.

## Hand checklist (for the user)

Start the game with `godot --path .` from the repo at the `story: E4-S8` commit. After each item, one more question: **what surprised you?**

1. **Par 3 under $1,500.** Click New course (not New sandbox, so building costs money). In the starting block pick par 3, paint a tee box, place the tee marker, then place the cup on plain rough before painting any green. Read the hole panel. Then paint a green round the cup and a fairway between, and Open the hole.
   - Did the hole panel tell you what was wrong (no cup yet; cup not on green) clearly enough to fix it without guessing?
   - What did the hole cost you (the balance before and after)? Did that feel cheap, about right, or dear for a par 3?
2. **Play it.** Switch to Play and play the hole.
   - Did the ball land where the aim line said it would?
   - What did the swing bar feel like?
3. **Open it, watch three guests.** Back in Build, Open the hole, run the day (1x or 3x) and watch three guests play it. Read their lines in the feed.
   - Did their reactions match your own view of the hole?
   - The script's guests called a plain par 3 "fine, but nothing in the way, nothing to think about", and the stars fell from 3 to 2.5 by the next morning, so fewer guests came (18 to 13). Did you notice the drop, and did it feel fair for a first hole?
4. **Par 5 with water.** Buy the land you need, build a par 5 of 471 yd or more with water across the direct line to the green.
   - What did it cost, land and building? Does that feel fair against what a day of guests pays you?
5. **Play it twice.** Once laying up short of the water, once going for it.
   - Was the choice real: did each play tempt you, and did either feel like the obvious one?
6. **Two days at 3x.** Press 3 and let two full days run (about 3 real minutes each), with the feed open in Play.
   - Could you read the feed at that speed? (The script measured up to 14 to 16 lines in the busiest 10 seconds on the nine.)
   - Did you see the stars move during a day, and did that read as the course's story or as noise?
7. **Fee at the ceiling.** In Build, drag the fee slider to its end (the ceiling). Read its tooltip, then run to the next morning and look at the morning line and the guests out.
   - Did arrivals drop in a way you noticed (on the nine: 22 guests at $60, 15 at the $87 ceiling)? Where did you notice it: the tooltip, the morning line, the course looking emptier?
   - Was the fee worth it, by your own feel, against the guests lost?
   - Leave the fee at a value and watch a day: if the stars dip during the day, the fee comes down with the ceiling and stays down when they recover (Finding 1). Did you notice? Should it stay down, go back up to what you set when the stars recover, or should the stars shown move less within a day?
8. **Save, quit, load.** Part way through a day, after building something and moving the fee, press F5. Note the day, the balance, the fee, the stars and the last feed lines. Quit the game. Start it again and press F9.
   - Is anything lost or different: the course, the balance, the fee, the stars, the day, the feed?
   - The load resumes at that day's morning and replays it. Did that read as right, or as losing time?
