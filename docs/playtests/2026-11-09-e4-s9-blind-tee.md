# E4-S9: the blind-tee rule on hilly land (2026-11-09)

E4-S1's economy sim found PlaytestNine's holes 1 to 8 blind on every reaction (384 of 384), so the nine rated 2 stars under 5/3/1. This pass measured what blocks each hole's sight line (`guests/hole_checks.gd`, D43), changed the rule where it was wrong (D55), and fixed the nine where it really is blind.

## What blocked each hole (before)

The eye is 1.75 yd above the tee and the mark 2.0 yd above the target. Trees block up to 10 yd above their tile. Clearance is the line's height above the ground; a negative clearance means the ground hides the line. **Finding 1.** The smooth ground does not cause the problem. It moves the worst clearance by at most 0.5 yd, and it flips no hole's verdict under either target. The check still read terrace tops after D50, which is inconsistent with what the player sees. D55 now reads `drawn_steps`.

**Finding 2.** The real defect was the dogleg target. D47 kept the fair check on the straight line. Since D54, guests aim their tee shots at the turn (`turn_point`), so the line ran to a point inside the bend that nobody plays to. The fixture fills each bend with trees or a hill, so every one of its four doglegs was blind by construction. D55 aims the line at the turn.

**Finding 3.** The rest of the nine is blind for real. It was laid out on hilly land without looking at sight lines. Aimed at the turn, hole 3 is seen. Holes 1 and 5 are hidden by ground (2.5 and 1.4 yd), and hole 6 by one land tree. The rule change alone gives 2 of 9 holes seen from the tee (1 before).

## The fixes

- **Rule (D55):** a dogleg's sight line runs to its turn, and every line runs over the drawn ground. The 2 yd mark and the 1.75 yd eye are unchanged, and there is no new tunable. A fairway edit re-measures a hole only when it moves the turn.
- **Nine (`tests/fixtures/playtest_nine.gd`, `courses/playtest_nine.json`):**
  - Tee lifts: hole 4 four steps (0 before), hole 5 three steps (0 before), hole 7 two steps (1 before).
  - The land trees on the lines of holes 2 and 6 are cleared (painted rough).
  - Holes 1 and 8 stay blind on purpose. Hole 1 turns round its hill, behind trees. Hole 8 plays over the south hill's crest, 5 yd above the line, which no tee lift clears.

After: 7 of 9 holes are seen from the tee (`tests/test_playtest_nine.gd`).
