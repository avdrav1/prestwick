# Devlog #4: Bend it round the corner

Title: Prestwick 0.8.0: shape your shots

A playtester put it plainly: "there's no advantage to a slice that makes the ball curve. The courses are straight, even the doglegs." They were right. Until now a mistimed swing only pushed the ball off line, and every ball flew dead straight. You could aim at a dogleg's corner, but you couldn't bend the ball round it.

0.8.0 fixes that.

## Pick a shape

Before you swing, press **Q** or **E** to pick a shape: **Hook, Draw, Straight, Fade or Slice** (named for a right-handed golfer). The ball now curves in the air. A draw starts right of where you aim and curves back left; a fade does the opposite. A hook or slice bends three times as far.

With a driver, a draw or fade bends about 12 yards and a hook or slice about 35. Shorter clubs bend less, and the putter never does. A draw runs on a little farther when it lands, a fade sits down a little sooner.

Shaping isn't free: the accuracy zone on the swing bar shrinks for a draw or fade, and more for a hook or slice. Miss it and you'll know.

The aim preview shows the curved flight, with a faint straight guide line, so you can aim at the trees and watch the arc bend back to the fairway before you swing.

## Guests shape too

Good golfers now play a draw or fade round a dogleg when the line is safe, and they'll tell you when it came off: "...and the draw round the corner was worth the risk." Weaker golfers sometimes curve a drive they meant to hit straight, and, as on any real course, it's usually a slice.

## Doglegs that reward it

Dogleg Hollow and Dogleg Hollow (Championship) have been retouched so a shaped tee shot is the right play on most corners. A straight drive struck long now runs through the corner into a bunker; a ball that curves with the hole stays on the fairway. Fourteen of the eighteen holes reward a draw or a fade. If you saved a game on one of them, the menu will tell you a newer version is out.

## Also in this build

The Famous courses page is tidier: the two eighteens and the five nines are grouped, and the list scrolls with the keyboard. The F1 key list now fits on screen in two columns.

Try it on Dogleg Hollow's 2nd: aim at the trees right of the corner, pick Fade, and watch it come back.
