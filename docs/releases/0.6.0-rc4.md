# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc4 (playtest build)

- Cart paths now matter: guests walk half again as fast on a path, and slower through trees, sand and water and up hills, and they take a path whenever it is quicker.
- A long walk from a green to the next tee (or from the clubhouse) with no path draws a complaint that names it, "a long walk from the 2nd green to the 3rd tee, 98 yd and no cart path"; a path that made the walk quick can draw praise. Pace complaints now count only waiting.
- The example course has paths on its three long walks.
- Known issues: the economy, the sound and music levels, and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot. `how-to-play.md` (attached) explains the game; `playtest_nine.json` is a nine-hole course to import.
- Next, in 0.6.0: the MVP, once ten outside playtesters have played and their findings are fixed.
