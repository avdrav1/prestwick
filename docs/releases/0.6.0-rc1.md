# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc1 (playtest build)

- The first build for outside playtesters: final generated art for every surface, tree, golfer, the clubhouse and decor, and sound for every shot, landing and guest reaction, with volumes on F2.
- A main menu (Esc) with new course, save and load, and course export and import: send a friend your course as one file; it opens with $5,000 to spend.
- Everything can be played from the keyboard; F1 lists every key. `how-to-play.md` beside the game explains the rest.
- Known issues: the economy, the sounds and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the golfer art is stiff in profile; skipping a busy day takes 10 to 15 seconds; the Windows build has not been launch-tested; the name "Prestwick" has not had its trademark check, so this build is for invited testers only, not a public post.
- Next, in 0.6.0: the MVP, once ten outside playtesters have played this build and their findings are fixed.
