# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc2 (playtest build)

- New music by avdrav: "Scaffold" while you build and "Progress" while you play, crossfading as you switch.
- Skip to morning (M) now takes a couple of seconds instead of ten or more, and ends the day exactly as playing it through would.
- A fuller, livelier golfer with a real stride and swing, flags that wave, and the green view's pin, cup and ball drawn to match the course.
- Dragging and undoing in Build stay smooth on a full nine.
- Known issues: the economy, the sounds and music levels, and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot. `how-to-play.md` (attached) explains the game; `playtest_nine.json` is a nine-hole course to import.
- Next, in 0.6.0: the MVP, once ten outside playtesters have played and their findings are fixed.
