# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc5 (playtest build)

- A goal checklist on every new course walks you through it: place a tee and a cup, paint a green, watch the hole open and guests pay, buy land, build a second hole, reach 3 stars. Hide folds it to a "Next:" line; Goals in the menu brings it back. Play hole waits until you have a hole to play.
- Aiming holds still: the camera frames the shot once and only glides when the aim would leave the screen.
- The swing bar explains itself: it is marked in yards, the green zone is labelled "accuracy", the readout shows the carry as you set power with the landing marker moving to match, and your first swing gets a short how-to.
- Water finds its level: painting water fills the hollow it is in, flat, up to its lowest rim, and refuses a hilltop ("water would run off: dig a hollow first").
- Known issues: filling a very large lake in one click can stutter for a frame; the economy, the sound and music levels, and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot. `how-to-play.md` (attached) explains the game; `playtest_nine.json` is a nine-hole course to import.
