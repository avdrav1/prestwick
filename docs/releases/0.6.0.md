# Prestwick 0.6.0

The first full release: build a golf course, then play it.

- Build on rolling land: shape the ground, paint fairways, greens, bunkers and water that finds its own level, lay cart paths and footbridges, and place up to nine holes, doglegs included. A ruler shows each hole's length and par as you build it, and a hole opens the moment it is legal.
- Guests come out of your clubhouse, pay a green fee, walk your paths, and say what they think of every hole; at dusk their verdicts set your stars, which bring more guests and a higher fee. Money paces how fast you grow.
- Play any hole yourself with a three-click swing bar marked in yards, a landing marker, and a top-down green view for putts.
- Five famous courses are built in (Harbor Links, Old Grey Links, Pine Ridge, Cliffside and Dogleg Hollow), each nine holes modelled on famous real holes, and they update with the game.
- A goal checklist for your first course, morning autosaves, course export to share with friends, full keyboard play, generated art, and original music by avdrav.
- Known issues: the Windows build has not yet been launch-tested on a machine without Godot; the economy, sound levels and keyboard-only play still await a hand playtest; playtesting continues after this release.
- Next: 18-hole courses.
