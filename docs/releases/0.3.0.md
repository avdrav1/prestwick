# Prestwick 0.3.0

- Start a new course on a generated map with hills and water, and a land budget to spend: buy land in parcels, then build your holes on what you own.
- The map shows what you own, where each parcel is and what the next one costs, and land you have not bought is out of bounds when you play, with a white-stake line on the aim that shows where a shot would go out.
- Hills play their way: the ball climbs, runs downhill and bounces on slopes, long par 4s and par 5s need room for a landing zone, and a hole's par checks its real length.
- Known issues: the tee still defaults to the longest club even when it flies past the cup, a one-parcel corridor makes a par 4 tight, all art is placeholder (the two-letter tags on each tile are surface labels), and the Windows build has not been launch-tested.
- Next, in 0.4.0: nine holes and the guests who play them, with complaints that point to the hole to fix.
