# Prestwick 0.5.0

- Build on a budget: start with $5,000 that pays for land, paint and shaping, earn green fees from every guest, and pay $5 a day for each open hole. Undo gives back what it charged.
- Your course earns a star rating from what guests say, set each evening; more stars bring more guests and let you charge more, with a fee slider in Build.
- Every course has a clubhouse: guests arrive there, walk to the first tee and walk back after their round. Move it with the Clubhouse tool in Build.
- Skip to the next morning (M), and save (F5) and load (F9) your game; a load picks up at the morning of the saved day.
- Known issues: all art is placeholder (the clubhouse too), the economy has been tuned by simulation but not yet by a person's hand playtest, skipping a busy day takes 10 to 15 seconds, and the Windows build has not been launch-tested.
- Next, in 0.6.0: final art and sound, course export and import, full keyboard play, and the first outside playtesters.
