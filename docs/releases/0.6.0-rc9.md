# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc9 (playtest build)

- No more walking on water: guests walk round ponds and creeks by the quickest dry route.
- Bridges: paint Cart path over water to build a wooden footbridge ($31 a tile). Guests cross on it; a ball that lands on it is still in the water.
- A hole guests can't walk to (an island green with no bridge, say) closes with a reason: "no way to walk to the 9th green: add a bridge".
- The four example courses now have bridges where they need them, including the walk out to Harbor Links' island green.
- Known issues: the economy, sound levels and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot.
