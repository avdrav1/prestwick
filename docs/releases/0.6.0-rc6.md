# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc6 (playtest build)

- More room to start: a new course now begins on a 4 x 4 parcel block, 320 yd on a side and about 445 yd corner to corner, so a full par 3 or a par 4 fits from the first hole. Land is now bought when the course outgrows the block, usually for its first par 5.
- Prices rose to match, so money still paces the game: a flat parcel is $800 and painting and shaping cost a little more (a basic par 3 is about $1,300).
- The goal checklist and the first-hole hint fit the bigger block (a par 3 of 120 to 300 yd, or a par 4 corner to corner).
- Your saved games keep the land they already own; the bigger block is for new courses.
- Known issues: a first hole built far from the clubhouse draws "long walk" complaints until it gets a cart path; the economy, sound levels and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot.
