# Prestwick 0.2.0

- Start a sandbox, place a tee and a cup, pick a par, and the game tells you on the map what stops the hole from opening.
- Play the hole in one click: aim with the mouse or arrows, pick a club, and swing with the three-click bar. The marker shows where a perfect swing lands, a blocked club says so, and the putter shows the putt at the pace that reaches the cup.
- Every surface plays its own way: rough, bunkers, trees, cart paths, water and out of bounds with penalty drops, and a cup that holds a ball arriving slowly enough.
- A scorecard counts strokes and picks you up at double par; the camera follows the ball and frames the shot while you aim.
- Known issues: a few steep greens and long slopes still take a little longer to settle than the target, the tee defaults to the longest club even when it flies past the cup, all art is placeholder, and the Windows build has not been launch-tested.
- Next, in 0.3.0: buy land in parcels on a generated map and build your course on it.
