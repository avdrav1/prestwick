# Prestwick 0.8.0

Shape your shots.

- Pick a shape before you swing with Q and E: Hook, Draw, Straight, Fade or Slice. The ball now curves in flight: about 12 yd for a draw or fade with the driver, 35 yd for a hook or slice, less with shorter clubs. A draw runs a little farther, a fade a little less; shaped shots narrow the accuracy zone. The aim preview and landing marker show the curved flight. Straight plays exactly as before.
- Guests shape too: skilled golfers draw or fade round doglegs when the line is safe (and say so when it comes off); weaker golfers sometimes slice a drive.
- Dogleg Hollow and Dogleg Hollow (Championship) are version 2: run-through bunkers past 14 corners mean a straight drive struck long runs into sand while a ball curving with the hole stays in. Saved games say a newer version is available.
- The Famous courses page groups the eighteens and the nines and scrolls with the keyboard; the F1 key list fits on screen in two columns.
- Known issues: the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot; at 0.5x zoom a draw or fade's arc is gentle and reads mostly against the straight guide line.
