# Prestwick 0.4.0

- Build up to nine holes, including doglegs, on smooth rolling ground with contour lines on every green and across the course (T toggles them).
- Guests play your course all day, in Build as well as Play, at 1x or 3x or paused. They aim round a dogleg's corner, and every finished hole earns a line in the feed and a tag on the map that says what they loved or what to fix.
- Click a tag or a feed line to jump to the hole. The hole panel says why guests complained and how many said it, and lines from before your last edit fade as history.
- Putt from a green view that turns so the putt runs up the panel, with a tick on the putter's bar at the power the aim marker needs. An arrow at the screen's edge points to an off-screen cup.
- Known issues: all art is placeholder, guests finish their day early so the course is often empty for its last third, the green view can grow large on diagonal putts, and the Windows build has not been launch-tested.
- Next, in 0.5.0: the economy, with green fees, upkeep and money to spend on the course.
