# Prestwick 0.6.0-rc3 (playtest build)

- A hole opens itself the moment it is legal ("Hole 1 is open: guests will play it"); Close keeps a hole shut until you reopen it. If the morning's guests found nothing open, they come as soon as your first hole opens.
- Building shows how long a hole is: with the Cup tool a ruler from the tee reads "285 yd: par 3", rings round the tee mark a driver's carry and roll and where par 4 and par 5 start, and a new course suggests how long to make a first par 3. Every club's distance is in F1 and in how-to-play.
- Saving is safe: F5 and F9 ask before they overwrite or replace a game, the game autosaves every morning (the last three mornings are kept), and Load lists your save and each autosave.
- Known issues: the economy, the sound and music levels, and keyboard-only play have not yet had a person's hand check; the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot. `how-to-play.md` (attached) explains the game; `playtest_nine.json` is a nine-hole course to import.
- Next: cart paths that guests walk faster on, and guests who comment on long walks.
