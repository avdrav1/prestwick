# Prestwick 0.1.0

- Paint and shape terrain on an isometric map: raise and lower land, and paint surfaces.
- See every slope, including rises that face away from the camera.
- Fire a debug shot and watch the ball fly, land and roll to rest on the tile the game predicted.
- Putts on greens bend across contours and break, and a ball settles on a gentle green slope instead of creeping for half a minute.
- Known missing: there is no swing and no clubs yet; the debug shot is the only way to hit a ball, and all art is placeholder.
- Known issues: steep greens and rough gullies can still take a long time to settle, and the Windows build has not been launch-tested because Wine is not available on the build machine.
- Next, in 0.2.0: one hole end to end, from placing a tee and cup to swinging, holing out and reading your score.
