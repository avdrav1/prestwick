# Prestwick 0.7.0

Eighteen holes.

- New courses start on a map 960 yards a side and hold up to eighteen holes; the hole list splits into Out and In once you have a hole 10.
- With all eighteen open the day runs longer and guests choose nine or eighteen: eighteens stop at the clubhouse at the turn and pay 1.75 times the nine-hole fee; nines start on the back when the 1st tee is busy.
- The economy paces the back nine: a 3-star course opens its nine around day 27 and its eighteen around day 69. Prices dropped a little to make room (a flat parcel is $700).
- A sixth famous course, Old Grey Links (Championship): the links nine plus a back nine of famous links holes.
- Faster everywhere: building, undo, the farthest zoom (now with a 0.1x step that shows the whole map) and skip to morning hold their frame budgets on a full eighteen, and a new course draws in under a second.
- Known issues: the Windows build has not been launch-tested on a machine without Godot; playtesting continues.
