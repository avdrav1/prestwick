E3-S75: scripts/nine_holes_perf.gd's D52 gate, with a display (RTX 3080), 24 logical cores, alone on the machine (D52; the desktop, a browser and a chat client open). Every run is a display run; `uptime` was read before each, and the script prints the load at its start and end (in the tables: start / end). Two runs went past load 4 from outside jobs (marked WARNING; the gate printed a warning and exited 0); they count as failures below.

Gated frames are the frames past frame_guest_ms_max 10 ms on the lattice and flooded days and in Play at 3x (the 60 fps headless days run only headless).

Verdict on the A/B: noise near the line, on both sides alike, not a regression and not a display-only cost of E3-S74.
- E3-S74's guest changes (the cart path cache and the statics key) only make steps cheaper, and the gated frames are Ball work between steps (below). The lattice and flooded days are the bare nine's land (NineHoles.land(7)) whatever the fixture, and they give 8 to 15 of each side's 10 to 17 gated frames. Both sides fail about as often: 6 runs of 10 pass on A, 5 of 10 on B (B better on the nine, worse on doglegs, within a run's spread of 1 to 5 frames: 24 gated frames on A, 27 on B).
- Ball tick cost has not moved since E3-S34 (best of 3 whole days, headless, the same ticks on every commit, so the same days): E3-S34 bebde86 26.4 and 26.2 us a tick (days 1 and 2), before E3-S70 26.3 and 26.2, bed8a77 26.2 and 26.0, 390089e 26.3 and 26.1.
- E3-S70's GuestHole.turn_of is not in them: it runs in a step (a group's first guest reaching the tee). Of the 51 gated frames before, none of the 5 in Play ran a step, and of the 46 on the days 1 measured a hole's statics and at most 1 ran a step (the days' frames that ran a step are worst 9.3 ms, once 10.07).

== cause ==
A scratch profile of the lattice and flooded days (headless; GuestBrain.advance_real and serve replayed with each guest's GuestHole.advance, each Ball.step, each plan launch and judge and each PuttAim.advance timed) puts every frame past 7 ms in one shape: a full frame of Ball work (200 ticks, or 133 putt ticks at weight 1.5) at about 35 us a tick is already 7 ms, and one tick in it took 1 to 5 ms. Those ticks were ordinary ones (a flight in mid-air, a roll on fairway or green, no hazard, drop, scatter or rest), which normally take 25 to 60 us. A control with no guest code (fixed 35 us chunks of GDScript arithmetic for 60 s, headless, load 1.2) saw the same stalls: 26 chunks of 1.0 to 5.3 ms in 3.3 million (about one every 2.3 s), median 18 us, p999 38 us. So the gated frames are the thread losing 1 to 5 ms to the machine inside a frame that already carries 7 ms of guests (more with a display). D52 bounded a lone outlier at 10.7 ms; a full frame plus a stall past 3 ms is past 10, and past 5.5 ms past 12.5.

== fix ==
The work, not the gate: make a full frame cheaper so a stall fits under 10 ms. A Ball tick is call-bound: CourseData.get_height (0.22 us) was three GDScript calls (get_height, in_bounds, _index), HeightField.height_at (1.9 us) makes four, gradient_at (3.8 us) eight, and a tick reads them dozens of times. get_height, get_surface and is_owned now spell out the bounds check and the index (is_owned the parcel index too, with parcels_wide's ceiling as integer division): the same integer reads, so every Ball result is bit-identical (the same ticks, 504,113 and 493,735, on days 1 and 2), and a tick costs 20.5 to 20.7 us where it cost 26.0 to 26.3 (-21%). Tried and dropped: one CourseData call reading height_at's four corners (no gain: its conditionals cost what the calls did). D52's thresholds and frame_guest_ms_max are unchanged.
After: the days' guest work p95 3.4 to 3.9 ms a frame (4.4 to 5.1 before, side B), Play at 3x's guest work p95 4.1 to 5.0.
