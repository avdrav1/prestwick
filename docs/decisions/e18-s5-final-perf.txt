== the two fixes ==
1. The D52 gate on the eighteen failed (headless, 1 frame of 13.94 ms, past 12.5: exit 1). Since E18-S3 the eighteen-hole day lets every eighteen finish, and on copies=2 the 18th green is 1,200 yd from the clubhouse across the map's water: a group of two guests who had picked up near the 18th tee started their walks home in one step (second 300 of day 1), each a dry search (D69) of about 6 ms (A* 2.9 ms over 410 tiles, straightening 2.7 ms), 12 ms in one step. Fix (guests/guest_brain.gd, guests/guest_walk.gd): a guest done with its hole ahead of its group has its next walk's dry route searched in the second it is done (GuestBrain._prefetch_early, once a position; the next walk's goal by _walk_goal_after: the door home or at the turn, else the next tee), and a group about to walk searches its guests' dry routes side by side on WorkerThreadPool (GuestWalk.prefetch_dry, two or more). Only the route cache is filled: every walk starts as before and reads its route from it. That frame 14.2 -> 9.0 to 9.6 ms; the headless gate 0 frames past 10 ms (worst 9.20, 8.98).
2. A cart path drag on the championship ran 15.4 / 16.9 to 17.0 ms a frame (median / p95) and its undo frame 19.8 to 20.8 ms: GuestSim's flush of path edits (ReactionFeed.walks_edited, every frame a path tile is laid) read the path grid a second time and each of the 18 round walks' 19 x 19 squares round its ends tile by tile through CourseData (GuestWalk.path_edit_reaches): 7.5 ms a frame. Fix: walks_edited takes the path grid GuestSim has just read, and path_edit_reaches reads the squares from the grid's bytes: 0.75 ms. Drag frames 8.8 / 9.7 to 9.9, undo frame 12.3 to 12.6 / 13.1 to 13.3, undo call 11.8 -> 4.4 ms.
Same results: a digest of every guest's position, state and walk (points, time, path) every second and every record, days 1 to 3 on five courses (copies=2, the eighteen fixture, the championship, the playtest nine, Harbor Links), is byte for byte the same before (HEAD's guest_brain.gd and guest_walk.gd) and after (below). tests/test_guest_walk.gd: prefetched dry routes are the routes searched alone, and a route after them gives the same walk; path_edit_reaches from the grid answers as the tile-by-tile reading at 300 random walks and edits with scattered path and bridges. Validator untouched (verdicts as before). Every skip PASS (the skipped day ends as the day run alone).
Not needed: the release frame's p95 is 11.7 to 12.7 ms on a quiet machine (16.5 was load 5), so the issue boxes and the hole panel's refresh were left as they are.
Also fixed in nine_holes_perf: the headless days' "on time" count read day_s (540) on an eighteen-hole day (1020 s), so it printed 88.8% late; it reads the brain's own day now (20432 frames, 20420 on time).
