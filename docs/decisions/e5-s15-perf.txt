E5-S15: scripts/nine_holes_perf.gd with a display (RTX 3080), 24 logical cores, after E5-S2/S3's final art (1,752 trees on the bare nine), D52. uptime was read before each run; the script prints the load at its start and end (below).

Cause: TreeView.refresh_cells rebuilt every depth row within 2 of each edited tile, and a drag emits one tiles_changed batch per brush step, so a drag_to frame rebuilt a few hundred rows (about 0.17 ms each) and an undo every row its tiles crossed. Fix: trees redraw tile by tile, only where a tile turned to or from trees or a trees tile's height moved (a tree's foot is its own tile's height, the drawn ground at a land tile's centre); the decor redraws only when a height or water moved. Screenshots before and after (tests/fixtures/nine_hazards_shot.tscn, NINE_AT=tee, 0.175x and 1x) are pixel-identical (magick compare -metric AE: 0).
