# Devlog #2: Five famous courses, built in

Title: Five famous courses, built in

Prestwick has always been about building your own course. But a blank map is a hard place to start, and it's hard to know what "good" looks like until you've played something good. So the game now ships with five courses of its own, each nine holes, and every hole is modelled on a famous real hole: its real par, its real yardage, and the one feature that made it famous.

You'll find them in the menu: press **Esc** and choose **Famous courses**. Pick one and you start a fresh game on it with $5,000 in the bank, a fully built course, and guests on their way.

## The five courses

**Harbor Links**: water, water everywhere. Creeks across the fairways, a lake down the side of a long par 4, and a finish that every golfer will recognise: a short par 3 to an island green, with a wooden footbridge the only way out to it.

**Old Grey Links**: the seaside game. Rolling dunes, deep pot bunkers, two winding burns, a road-hole par 4 with a nasty little bunker at the green, a tiny "postage stamp" par 3 ringed with sand, and a home hole with a hollow in front of the green.

**Pine Ridge**: tree-lined parkland. Every hole plays down its own corridor of pines, with a sweeping par 5 round a creek, a short par 3 over the water, a field of furrowed "church pew" bunkers, and an uphill finish to the clubhouse.

**Cliffside**: clifftops round an island. A tiny par 3 out on a point, a long par 4 whose approach crosses a chasm, a par 3 that carries a bay, and a coastline par 5 to finish.

**Dogleg Hollow**: added after a playtester pointed out that hardly any of the holes turned. Every hole is a famous dogleg, bending 50 to 80 degrees round a lake, a pond, a wall of trees or a great sea of sand, plus a double-dogleg par 5. Cutting the corner is always tempting, and always a gamble.

The courses have original names, and so do all the holes. We credit the real holes privately, but we'd rather you discovered them on your own: if you know your golf, you'll spot most of them on the first tee.

## Built in, and kept up to date

These courses are part of the game, not files you download. Every time the game updates, the courses update with it. When guests started walking round water instead of across it, all five got the footbridges they needed. When a bridge audit found balls being walked 140 yards round a chasm, that hole got a bridge right on the line. You get those fixes without doing anything.

If you've saved a game on one of them, your save keeps the course exactly as you left it, with your edits, holes and money. When a newer version of a course is out, the menu tells you, and you can start fresh on it whenever you like.

## Play them, then build your own

The famous courses rate about four and a half stars with guests, so they're a good way to see what a great course feels like: where guests praise the risk and reward, and where a long walk without a cart path makes them grumble. Then press **New course** and try to beat them.

If you build something you're proud of, export it (Esc, Export course) and share it in the comments. I'd love to play it.

Download the latest build on this page (Windows and Linux; the itch app keeps it updated), and tell me what you think: in the comments, or on GitHub at https://github.com/avdrav1/prestwick/issues

See you on the first tee.
