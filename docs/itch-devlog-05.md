# Devlog #5: Opening shot

Title: Prestwick 0.9.0: an opening flyover

The first time you start Prestwick now, it shows you what the game is before it asks you to do anything.

## Thirty seconds at dawn

The game opens at dawn over Dogleg Hollow (Championship). The light warms into day as the camera comes down over the course and glides along the closing holes. A golfer walks off the 17th green to the 18th tee, where the fairway runs up a narrow avenue of trees and turns right at the corner.

Then the camera pulls back to show what they're up against. The aim line points straight into the trees on the left. They swing, the ball starts left, and as it climbs, time slows. You watch it slice back across the avenue and out round the corner, landing on the fairway well past the turn. Then the title.

Nothing in the intro is a video or new art. It's the game's own course, guest and ball, and that drive is a real slice, simulated exactly as it would be if you hit it. The slow motion only slows down how it's shown.

## Once, then on demand

The intro plays the first time you launch the game. After that you go straight to the menu, and **Esc, Watch intro** plays it again whenever you like. Any key or click skips it.

## Try the shot yourself

Load Dogleg Hollow (Championship) from Famous courses, go to the 18th, pick **Slice** with **E**, and aim at the left trees.
