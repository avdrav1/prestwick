Short description: Build a golf course, then play it. Your guests will tell you what they think.

Genre: Simulation. Tags: golf, management, building, sandbox, isometric, pixel art, singleplayer.

## Long description

**Prestwick** is a golf course you build, then play.

Start with $5,000 and a patch of rolling land. Shape the ground, paint fairways, greens, bunkers and ponds, and drop a tee and a cup. The moment a hole is legal it opens, and guests walk out of your clubhouse to play it.

Every guest pays a green fee and tells you what they thought. A blind tee shot, a hole with nothing to think about, a long walk with no cart path: they'll say so in the feed and on the map, and at dusk their verdicts set your stars. More stars bring more guests and let you charge more. Money paces how fast you grow, one hole and one parcel of land at a time, toward a full nine.

And it's your course, so play it. Pick a club, aim, and swing with a three-click bar. The ball follows the slopes you shaped, putts break on the greens you contoured, and the green view shows every slope.

**What's in this build**

- Build on smooth, rolling ground: raise, lower, and paint nine surfaces. Water finds its own level.
- Up to nine holes: par 3s, 4s and 5s, doglegs included. A ruler shows each hole's length and par as you place it.
- Guests who play your course, wait their turn, walk your cart paths and footbridges (they never wade: paint cart path over water to bridge it), and comment on every hole.
- An economy with green fees, upkeep, land to buy, and a star rating set each evening.
- Play any hole yourself, with a readable swing bar, a landing marker, and a top-down green view for putts.
- A goal checklist that walks you through your first course.
- Autosave every morning; F5 and F9 ask before they overwrite.
- Four famous courses built in, to play or rebuild, each nine holes modelled on famous real holes: Harbor Links (water and an island green), Old Grey Links (seaside links), Pine Ridge (tree-lined parkland) and Cliffside (clifftops over the sea). Famous courses in the menu starts a new game on one, and each update brings their latest version.
- Export a course as one file and send it to a friend.
- Fully playable from the keyboard (F1 lists every key).
- Original music by avdrav.

**Controls**

- Build tools are on the bottom bar. Tab switches between Build and Play.
- Swing: Space or click to start, again for power (distance), again in the green zone for a straight shot. Aim with the mouse or the arrow keys.
- P pauses the day, 1 and 3 set its speed, and M skips to the next morning.
- Esc opens the menu: new course, save, load, import and export, and sound.

**This is a playtest build**

Prestwick is in active development, and this build is for playtesters. Things will change, and some numbers aren't tuned yet. If something confuses you, breaks, or delights you, please tell us. Use the comments below, or open a report on GitHub: https://github.com/avdrav1/prestwick/issues

Runs on Windows and Linux. No install needed: download and run.
